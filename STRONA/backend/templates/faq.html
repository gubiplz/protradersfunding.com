Getting started
How do I start a challenge?
Pick a plan on the pricing section, create an account in the trader portal, fill in your details at checkout and pay the one-time fee. Your MT5 credentials are generated automatically and appear in your dashboard and inbox.
When do I get my MT5 account?
Automatically, once your payment is confirmed. Provisioning is fully automated, and no human on our side needs to click anything. Your dashboard shows the live status and the credentials also arrive by email.
Is there a free trial?
No. Challenges start at $299 for a $25,000 account, and the fee is a one-time payment refunded in full with your first payout. Coupon codes are accepted at checkout.
Are these live trading accounts?
No. All accounts are demo accounts with virtual funds running in a simulated environment on MT5 servers. You never trade real money; you earn performance-based rewards for meeting the objectives.
Can I use my own MT5 app?
Yes. The credentials work in MT5 on desktop, mobile and in the web terminal. Log in with the server, login and password from your dashboard.
Built-in Terminal, prices & fees
Where do the prices in the Built-in Terminal come from?
Every market in the terminal, from crypto and metals to oil, indices and FX pairs, uses live reference prices from Hyperliquid, a third-party venue we are not affiliated with. The order book and the tape show that venue, and every simulated fill is settled on its reference price. On Forex pairs and platinum, where Hyperliquid prints only a few trades a minute, the chart draws from a consolidated spot feed instead, so it can differ from the fill price by about the spread. Reference prices can differ from what a real broker would have quoted at the same moment.
Are my trades sent to Hyperliquid or any broker?
No. No order ever leaves our platform: the terminal keeps a simulated ledger and fills your order against the reference order book at the moment you click. Fills, balances and equity are all values inside the simulation. That is why what you earn is a performance reward, not a trading profit.
Do I need MT5 installed, or can I trade in the browser?
You can trade entirely in the browser. Open the terminal and sign in with the MT5 login, password and server from your dashboard email. The same credentials also work in MT5 on desktop, mobile and web if you prefer it. There is nothing to download either way.
What can I trade, and when are markets open?
The terminal lists 12 crypto pairs (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and more), gold, silver and platinum, WTI, Brent and natural gas, the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and Nikkei 225, and the EURUSD, GBPUSD and USDJPY pairs. Everything except crypto is closed on Saturday and Sunday server time and reopens Monday at 00:00 server time; weekend positions are marked at the Friday close and no take profit or stop loss fires until the open. Crypto trades around the clock Monday to Friday, and on weekends only on accounts with the Weekend Trading add-on ($199).
What commissions, spreads or swaps apply?
A commission of $9 per lot per side, charged when you open and again when you close. There is no markup on prices: the spread you see in the order book is the reference venue's own spread, and market orders fill through that book at the volume-weighted price with a default slippage limit of 1%. There are no swaps or overnight charges in the terminal. Pending orders, take profits and stop losses are checked on 1-minute candles, so they fire even when you are not watching.
What leverage do I get, and are there position size limits?
Leverage is up to 1:100 and fixed per market, so you do not choose it: Forex 1:100, metals and indices 1:50, energy 1:20, crypto 1:5 to 1:10. Margin is shared across the whole account, and if equity falls to 50% of used margin a stop-out closes all positions. Every account also has a cap on total open volume of 6 lots per $100,000 of account size (1.5 lots on $25,000, 6 on $100,000, 12 on $200,000), counted across the terminal and MT5 together. The terminal rejects an order that would go over the cap; the smallest order is 0.01 lot, in 0.01 steps.
Are there limits on the number of orders or open positions?
There is no cap on the number of pending orders. Positions are netted per symbol, so a second order on the same market adds to, reduces or reverses your existing position instead of opening a second one. The only hard limits are the total-volume cap above and a rate limit of 20 order actions per 10 seconds, which stops runaway scripts rather than normal trading.
Can I use MT5 and the Built-in Terminal on the same account at once?
Yes. Both show the same account: one balance, one set of rules, one exposure cap and one trading history. Sign in to the terminal with your MT5 login, password and server, and keep MT5 open at the same time if you like. On accounts that are tracked live from MT5 (for example with the Copytrading add-on), the terminal shows the account read-only and orders go through MT5.
Rules & objectives
How is the daily loss limit calculated?
Against your equity at the start of the trading day (server time), including floating P&L. The limit is 5% of the starting balance, not of that day's equity. Example on a $100,000 account: if the day starts at $102,000, your equity floor for that day is $97,000 ($102,000 minus $5,000). Crossing it fails the account automatically.
Static vs trailing drawdown — what's the difference?
Static drawdown is a fixed floor below the starting balance, e.g. $90,000 on a $100,000 account with a 10% limit, forever. Trailing drawdown instead follows your equity high-water mark, so the floor climbs as you profit. Every plan we sell uses static drawdown (10% on 2-Step, 8% on Instant Funding); we do not use trailing drawdown at all.
What happens when I break a rule?
The risk engine fails the account, open positions are closed and the exact breach reason (which rule, at what equity) is recorded in your dashboard. There are no partial penalties and no discretionary reviews — the same code applies to everyone.
Is news trading allowed? Weekend holding? EAs?
Yes to all three. There are no news blackouts, no weekend-flat requirement and no strategy bans beyond abuse of the simulation (see "What trading practices are not allowed?" below). The only rules are the published loss limits, profit targets, minimum trading days and the cap on total open volume.
Can someone else trade my account?
Yes, with our approval. Account management is allowed once we have approved the arrangement in writing, before any trading on your behalf starts. Write to {{ support_email }} from your registered address and name who will be trading. The account stays yours and so does responsibility for the rules: a limit breached by your manager is still a breach of your account, and rewards are paid only to you after KYC. Handing over credentials without an approved arrangement is prohibited and can trigger an account review. Full wording in Section 7 of the Terms.
What counts as a trading day?
Any calendar day (server time) on which you executed at least one trade. Minimum trading days per phase are listed on each plan card.
Is there a time limit on the evaluation?
No. Take as long as you need. The account stays active as long as you respect the loss limits.
What time does the daily loss limit reset?
At 00:00 server time, which is UTC+2. At that moment we take a new snapshot of your equity, including floating P&L, and the day's floor is that equity minus 5% of the starting balance. The floor stays fixed until the next midnight; it does not move up if you profit during the day. Trading days are counted on the same clock.
Is there a consistency rule?
No. There is no best-day rule, no lot-size consistency and no per-day profit cap. One big day can take you to the target as long as you also meet the minimum trading days and respect the loss limits. The only size rule is the cap on total open volume.
What exactly do I need to pass each phase?
On 2-Step, Phase 1 ends when your balance from closed trades reaches +10% and Phase 2 at +5%, each with at least 5 trading days. Throughout both phases your equity must stay above the daily floor (5% of the starting balance) and the static overall floor (10%). The target is measured on balance, so floating profit does not count until you close. Instant Funding has no evaluation and no target: the account is funded from day one.
What happens after I pass?
The next phase opens on the same login, with the balance, the floors and the trading-day counter reset to the starting values. After the last phase the account becomes funded, you get a certificate with a verifiable ID, and identity verification opens in your dashboard. Nothing is re-provisioned and no one on our side needs to approve the step.
Do the same rules apply once I'm funded (profit target, time limit, inactivity)?
There is no profit target and no time limit on a funded account. The 5% daily loss limit, the static overall drawdown (10% on 2-Step, 8% on Instant Funding) and the volume cap keep applying, and a breach closes the account. Before your first payout the funded account needs its own minimum trading days: 5 on 2-Step, 30 on Instant Funding. There is no inactivity rule; a quiet account only gets a reminder email.
What trading practices are not allowed?
Anything that makes money from the simulation instead of from the market. That means exploiting delayed, frozen or wrong prices, latency arbitrage against the feed, opposite positions across your own accounts or coordinated with other traders so that one side is guaranteed to pass, high-frequency order spam, and repeated all-in attempts where each challenge is bet on one maximum-risk trade. If you find a platform bug, report it to support instead of using it: results produced this way are voided and the account is closed under Section 6 of the Terms. Copying your own trades in the same direction between your accounts is allowed only with the Copytrading add-on.
Are EAs and bots allowed, also in the Built-in Terminal?
Yes in MT5: EAs, indicators and copiers run on your side and we do not inspect them. An EA is bound by the same limits as manual trading: the loss limits, the cap on open volume in lots, and market hours (crypto on weekends only with the Weekend Trading add-on). The Built-in Terminal has no EA or plugin layer and no public trading API, so there is no supported way to automate it; scripts hitting the order endpoints run into the rate limit. Whatever drives the orders, the prohibited-practices list above still applies to the result.
Can I trade from a shared device or network, and can I use a VPN?
You can trade from any device, as long as only you use your credentials. Every terminal sign-in is logged with IP address, approximate location, browser and device, and that log is part of the review before a payout, so sign-ins by several people or from several places will be asked about. Do not use a VPN, a proxy or any service that masks your location: sign-ins through them can lead to a review and to a reward being declined. The Copytrading add-on additionally records a written permission to use several devices and to copy between your own accounts.
Funded accounts & payouts
How do payouts work?
Payouts are on demand: on a funded account, request a payout from your dashboard whenever you have profit — there is no payout calendar and no minimum wait between requests. After a one-time KYC verification each request is reviewed by our team and paid out. Your share is 90% on evaluation plans and 70% on Instant Funding.
How long does a payout take?
Each request is reviewed individually by our risk team, who check the account, its trading history and your verification. Once a request is unlocked, you confirm the amount and add your vUSDT wallet in the dashboard (or confirm the saved one), the transfer goes out and you receive a verifiable payout certificate. The Express Payout add-on places your requests at the front of the review queue.
When do I get my challenge fee back?
The full fee is added to your first payout from the funded account.
How does the scaling plan work?
Each time a funded account grows by +15%, you choose from your dashboard: take the profit as a payout, or move up to the next account size in our pricing. If you move up, the profit pays for the upgrade and we set up a fresh account at the bigger size, funded from day one. There is no application process and you can repeat it all the way to $2,000,000.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes. Every passed evaluation and funded account gets a certificate with a unique ID that anyone can check on the verification page.
Is there a minimum payout amount?
Yes: $500 or 2% of the account size, whichever is higher. That is $500 on a $25,000 account, $2,000 on $100,000 and $20,000 on $1,000,000. Amounts are whole dollars, and you can request part of your available share instead of all of it.
What do I need before requesting a payout?
A funded account with profit from closed trades, approved identity verification (KYC), and the minimum trading days on that funded account: 5 on 2-Step, 30 on Instant Funding. The request must be at or above the minimum amount, and only one request per account can be open at a time. The sequence is then: you request an amount, our team reviews the account and unlocks the request, you confirm the amount and your vUSDT wallet, and the transfer is marked paid with a certificate.
Can I trade while my payout is under review?
Yes, the account stays open and all rules keep applying. The requested amount is checked again at approval: if your profit has dropped below it in the meantime, the request is rejected and you submit a new one for the lower amount. Many traders keep a buffer above the requested amount for exactly that reason.
What happens to my balance and drawdown after a payout?
The profit that paid for the payout (the payout amount divided by your split) is deducted from the balance. A full payout brings the account back to its starting balance; a partial one leaves the rest of the profit on it. Equity, the equity peak and the day-start baseline are set to the new balance, while the overall drawdown floor is static and does not move. The fee refund added to your first payout is on top and is not deducted.
Does a payout count against my daily loss limit?
No. The deduction is booked together with a new day-start baseline equal to the new balance, so the daily floor is recalculated from there and the payout itself can never trigger a breach. Only your trades after the payout count against the new floor.
Is there a cap on total payouts?
No. There is no cap on the number of payouts, on the total paid from one account, or on how often you request (there is no payout calendar). Each request is reviewed individually. Scaling is optional: at +15% you can choose to move up to the next account size instead of taking the payout.
What profit split applies to payouts on each plan?
90% on every 2-Step plan and 70% on Instant Funding, on all account sizes. The Split Boost add-on raises Instant Funding to 80%; it is not available on 2-Step. The Express Payout add-on does not change the split; it puts your requests at the front of the review queue.
Which networks or wallets can payouts be sent to?
Payouts go out in vUSDT, PTF's own token on the Polygon network, to a wallet you control. You add the Polygon wallet address in the dashboard when your request is unlocked, and it is saved for the next one. Changing a saved wallet requires a code we email to your registered address.
Billing & account
Is the fee one-time or recurring?
One-time, per challenge. No subscriptions or hidden costs, and it's refunded with your first payout. See the refund policy for details.
Do you accept coupon codes?
Yes. Enter the code at checkout and the discount is applied instantly.
Can I run multiple challenges?
Yes. Each account is evaluated independently against its own objectives.
What if I fail — can I try again?
Always. Failing a challenge has no consequences beyond that account ending, and you can start a new challenge at any time.
What payment methods do you accept, and can I pay in crypto?
Cards through Stripe at checkout; we never see your card details. Crypto is possible on request: write to {{ support_email }} with the plan you want, and your account is activated once we confirm the transfer. Store credits granted to your account are applied at the end of the checkout.
Can I get a refund before I start trading?
Only if your MT5 credentials have not been delivered yet. Delivery is automatic right after payment, so in practice that window is very short; once the credentials are in your dashboard the service counts as performed and the fee is non-refundable. Duplicate charges and accounts we could not provision are always refunded in full. See the Refund Policy.
Can I pay with someone else's card?
Pay with a card in your own name. The order must be placed from your own registration, and rewards go only to you after KYC; if the payer details do not match your identity documents, we may ask you to explain before a reward is approved. Contact us before opening a card dispute: unwarranted chargebacks end the account under the Terms.
Can I stack coupon codes?
No. One coupon code per order, and when a flash-sale offer is running the better single discount applies rather than both. The Upgrade Your Size promo code is a separate field and can be combined with a coupon. Add-ons are charged at full price, and store credits are applied last.
Account, KYC & support
Who can join, and is there an age requirement?
Anyone 18 or older who can legally enter into a contract, subject to the country restrictions below. One person holds one registration and can run as many challenges under it as they like. Details at registration and checkout must be your own, as they are used to open your MT5 account and later to verify your identity.
When do I need to complete KYC, and who handles it?
Verification opens in your dashboard once one of your accounts is funded and is required before the first payout request; if you want to verify earlier, support can open it for you. You enter your full name, country, date of birth, address and ID type and number, and upload your document in the portal. Our own team reviews it. It is a one-time step that covers all your accounts.
What personal data do you collect?
Account data (email, hashed password, name), checkout data (name and phone), KYC data, and trading telemetry of your simulated accounts. Built-in Terminal sign-ins are logged with IP, approximate location, browser and device, and kept for 12 months. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we store order metadata, never card numbers. Full details in the Privacy Policy.
Can I share my portal or MT5 login?
No. Credentials are personal, and sign-ins are checked against the registered owner before a payout is approved. The one exception is an account management arrangement approved in writing before trading starts (see "Can someone else trade my account?" above). The Copytrading add-on lets you use several devices and copy between your own accounts, but still not hand credentials to someone else.
Can I transfer or sell my account?
No. Buying, selling or transferring accounts is prohibited conduct under Section 6 of the Terms and voids the results. Accounts are tied to the registration that paid for them, and rewards are paid only to that verified person.
How do I contact support?
Fastest is the chat in your trader portal: an AI assistant answers rule and platform questions right away, and a real person from our team reads every message. Email {{ support_email }} for anything you prefer not to type in a chat. Payout requests, KYC and billing are handled from the portal itself. See also the contact page.
Where are your Terms, Privacy and Refund policies?
Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and Risk Disclosure, all linked in the footer of every page. The objectives for each plan are published on the Trading Objectives page. Challenges you already bought stay governed by the objectives shown at the time of purchase.
