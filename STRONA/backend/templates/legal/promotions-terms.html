Promotions and Giveaway Terms

Version {{ legal.version }} · Last updated: {{ legal.date }}

These terms cover discount codes, promotional offers, the newsletter giveaway and the free rewards in the trader portal. The organiser of every promotion is the operator named in Section 1 of the Terms of Service, of which these terms form part.

1. Discount codes and offers

  • A discount code reduces the price of a Challenge by the percentage shown when you apply it at checkout. The price after the discount is shown before you pay.
  • One code can be used per order. Where a code and a time-limited offer both apply, the one that gives you the lower price is used; they do not add up.
  • Personal codes (for example a code won in the portal or bought with loyalty points) work only on the account they were issued to and expire on the date shown with the code.
  • Codes have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money and cannot be applied to an order already paid.
  • "Buy one, get one" and "upgrade" promotions apply only while they are shown as active at checkout, and give exactly what the checkout summary says.
  • If an order bought with a discount is refunded, the refund is the amount you actually paid.

2. Newsletter

Subscribing to the newsletter means you agree to receive marketing e-mails from us. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every e-mail. If a discount code is offered for subscribing, it is sent to the address you subscribed with.

3. Newsletter giveaway

  • When a giveaway is running, the prize and the draw date are shown in the sign-up window.
  • No purchase is necessary. Entry is free: you enter by subscribing to the newsletter before the draw date. One entry per person.
  • You must be at least 18 and must not live in a restricted country.
  • The winner is picked at random from the people subscribed at the draw date, and is told by e-mail at the address they subscribed with.
  • The prize is a free Challenge of the size shown. It is not money and cannot be exchanged for money. The Challenge is governed by the Terms of Service like any other.
  • If the winner does not reply to the notification so that the account can be opened, the prize is not awarded to them.

4. Free rewards in the portal

  • The portal has free features that give rewards: the daily check-in, the daily reveal and the Trade Case. Taking part costs nothing and needs no purchase.
  • Rewards are discount codes, loyalty points or, where shown, a free Challenge. Which reward you get from the daily reveal and the Trade Case is decided at random. Most draws give a small reward or none.
  • Loyalty points and codes have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.
  • We may change or end these features. Rewards already granted stay valid until the expiry date shown with them.

5. Misuse

Creating several accounts to collect rewards or entries, or using automation to do so, is not allowed. We may cancel rewards and entries obtained that way.

6. Personal data

We use the e-mail address you give us to run the promotion and to tell the winner. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
