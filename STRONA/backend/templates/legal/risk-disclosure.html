1. Simulated trading only

All accounts provided by {{ site_name }} are demo accounts with virtual funds. All trading activity conducted through the Platform is performed in a simulated environment. No real money is at risk in the markets, no deposits are accepted, and no actual financial transactions occur. Any reference to capital, profit or loss describes values inside the simulation.

Reference prices come from third-party exchanges and market-data providers (including Hyperliquid, Binance and Bybit, depending on the instrument). We are not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of them, and your simulated account does not interact with any real account, order book or funds on any exchange.

2. Rewards are not guaranteed

Payments to traders are conditional, performance-based rewards derived from simulated results under our Terms of Service. They are not investment returns, and a reward request can be refused for the reasons listed in Section 5 of the Terms. When paid, rewards are issued in vUSDT on the Polygon network. By default you may request a reward every 14 calendar days on a funded account; the On-demand Payout add-on removes that wait between requests but does not guarantee approval or transfer time. Past performance — yours or anyone else's — does not guarantee future outcomes. Statistics, leaderboards, certificates and payout examples displayed on this website do not show what a typical participant achieves and are not a promise of future results. Across the evaluation industry, most people who buy an evaluation do not reach a reward.

vUSDT warning. vUSDT is {{ site_name }}'s own internal token on Polygon — not Tether USDT, not a stablecoin and not money. It is transferable to your wallet; we do not redeem it for cash or for any other currency or asset, and we do not operate or guarantee any market on which it can be exchanged. Its value is not guaranteed. You should assume that a reward paid in vUSDT may not be convertible into money. A dollar amount shown for a reward is the number of vUSDT, not an amount of US dollars. Holding or receiving vUSDT is not an investment product.

Token risks. A reward exists only as vUSDT, so the usual risks of a token apply to it. Transfers depend on the Polygon network, which can be congested or unavailable. A transfer to a wrong or mistyped address cannot be reversed, and neither can the loss of your wallet or its keys; we cannot recover either. Moving the token on from your wallet costs a network fee paid in Polygon's own token. There may be no one willing to exchange vUSDT for anything else.

3. The fee is at risk

The Challenge fee is a payment for access to an evaluation program. If you do not meet the trading objectives, the fee is not returned as money to your card (see the Refund Policy and the Right of Withdrawal). On 2-Step plans, where a first reward is approved on that account, the Challenge fee is credited as additional vUSDT with that payout; Instant Funding has no fee credit. Do not purchase a Challenge with money you cannot afford to spend.

4. Leveraged products are inherently risky

The simulation mirrors leveraged products: foreign exchange and CFDs on indices, metals, energy and cryptocurrencies. In real markets these instruments carry a high level of risk and can result in losses exceeding deposits; they are not suitable for everyone. Skills demonstrated in a simulation may not transfer to live market conditions, where slippage, liquidity, execution and psychology differ.

Nothing on this website (including educational content, statistics, leaderboards and certificates) constitutes investment advice, a personal recommendation, or a solicitation to buy or sell any financial instrument. {{ site_name }} is not a licensed broker or investment adviser and does not provide regulated financial services.

6. Technology risk

The simulation depends on our Built-in Terminal, on any third-party platform infrastructure used for your account and on third-party market data feeds. Outages, feed errors or maintenance windows can affect trading and account monitoring. Price errors and outages are handled under Section 11 of our Terms; the risk engine's recorded data is the basis for rule enforcement, and you can ask for it to be reviewed.
