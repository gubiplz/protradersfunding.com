1. Simulated trading only

All accounts provided by {{ site_name }} are demo accounts with virtual funds. All trading activity conducted through the Platform is performed in a simulated environment. No real money is at risk in the markets, no deposits are accepted, and no actual financial transactions occur. Any reference to capital, profit or loss describes values inside the simulation.

2. Rewards are not guaranteed

Payments to traders are discretionary, performance-based rewards derived from simulated results under our Terms of Service. They are not investment returns. Past performance — yours or anyone else's — does not guarantee future outcomes. Statistics displayed on this website are historical records of the platform and are not a promise of future results.

3. The fee is at risk

The Challenge fee is a payment for access to an evaluation program. If you do not meet the trading objectives, the fee is not returned (see the Refund Policy for the cases where it is). Do not purchase a Challenge with money you cannot afford to spend.

4. Leveraged products are inherently risky

The simulation mirrors leveraged products — foreign exchange and CFDs on indices, metals and cryptocurrencies. In real markets these instruments carry a high level of risk and can result in losses exceeding deposits; they are not suitable for everyone. Skills demonstrated in a simulation may not transfer to live market conditions, where slippage, liquidity, execution and psychology differ.

5. No investment advice

Nothing on this website — including educational content, statistics, leaderboards or certificates — constitutes investment advice, a personal recommendation, or a solicitation to buy or sell any financial instrument. {{ site_name }} is not a licensed broker or investment adviser and does not provide regulated financial services.
