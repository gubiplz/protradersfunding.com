Affiliate Program Terms

Last updated: November 2026

These Affiliate Program Terms ("Affiliate Terms") govern participation in the {{ site_name }} affiliate / referral program. They form part of the Terms of Service. If anything here conflicts with the Terms of Service on a point specific to affiliates, these Affiliate Terms control for that point.

1. Who can join

  • Every registered trader account includes a personal referral code and link in the portal under Rewards.
  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into contracts.
  • We may suspend or terminate affiliate participation where these Affiliate Terms or the Terms of Service are breached.

2. Commission

  • You earn 10% of the challenge fee actually paid on each eligible paid order placed by a trader attributed to your referral code.
  • Attribution is lifetime for that referred trader's paid challenge purchases under the same registration, not only the first order.
  • Commission is calculated on the fee paid after discounts and store credit applied at checkout. Add-ons that are part of the paid order are included in that paid amount when charged on the same order.
  • Unpaid, refunded, charged-back or cancelled orders do not earn commission. If a paid order is later refunded or charged back, we may reverse the related commission.

3. Attribution

  • A visitor who opens the site with your ?ref= link may have that code remembered (subject to cookie / consent settings) and attached at sign-up. The code can also be entered manually at registration.
  • The code stored on the referred trader's registration is what credits you on later purchases.
  • Self-referrals (using your own link or code on your own purchases) are not eligible.
  • Coupon-stacking abuse, fake accounts, incentive fraud and any attempt to manufacture referrals are not eligible and may void commission and close accounts.

4. How commission is claimed

  • Earned and unclaimed commission is shown in the portal under Rewards.
  • Claims are made only in the portal, as either:
    • Store credit — credited to your trader balance and usable toward challenge purchases on the Platform; or
    • vUSDT — {{ site_name }}'s native internal token on Polygon (not Tether USDT), queued to the Polygon wallet saved on your account (same wallet used for funded-account rewards). Transferable on Polygon; we do not redeem it for cash or fiat. Save a wallet under Payouts before choosing this option.
  • There is no cash payout of affiliate commission, and commission is not paid by emailing or messaging support.
  • Store credit from an affiliate claim is ordinary store credit: it is not withdrawable as cash and follows the same checkout rules as other credits.

5. Marketing and conduct

  • You may share your link honestly. Do not misrepresent the Platform as a broker, as real capital trading, or as guaranteed income.
  • Do not use spam, misleading ads, trademark bidding that impersonates {{ site_name }}, or content that violates applicable law.
  • We may require you to correct or take down materials that breach this section.

6. Changes and termination

  • We may change the commission rate, claim methods or these Affiliate Terms prospectively. Material changes will be announced on the Platform. Commission already earned under the prior rules remains claimable under the claim rules then in force, unless voided for abuse.
  • Either party may stop participating at any time. Unclaimed eligible commission remains claimable in the portal unless voided under Section 3 or the Terms of Service.

7. Contact

Questions about the affiliate program: {{ support_email }}. Claims themselves are submitted in the portal, not by email.
