1. Who we are and what we offer

{{ site_name }} ("we", "us", "the Platform") operates a trading skills evaluation service. We sell access to evaluation programs ("Challenges") conducted on demo trading accounts with virtual funds hosted on MetaTrader 5 infrastructure. We are a technology and education company: we are not a broker, investment firm, fund manager or financial adviser, and we do not accept deposits or execute real-market transactions on your behalf.

2. Simulated environment

All accounts provided under these Terms are demo accounts. All trading occurs in a simulated environment with fictitious funds. No real money is traded and your activity does not reach any real market. Amounts referred to as "capital", "balance", "profit" or "drawdown" describe values inside the simulation. Payments made to traders are discretionary performance-based rewards calculated from simulated results, not proceeds of real trading.

3. Eligibility and account registration

  • You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into contracts.
  • Information provided at registration and checkout (name, e-mail, phone) must be accurate — it is used, among other things, to open your MetaTrader 5 account.
  • One person may hold one registration; running multiple Challenges under it is allowed.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

4. Challenges, rules and enforcement

Each Challenge is governed by the objectives displayed on its plan card and on the Trading Objectives table at the time of purchase: profit targets, maximum daily loss, maximum overall loss (static or trailing), and minimum trading days. These rules are monitored and enforced automatically by our risk engine. If a rule is breached, the account is marked as failed and open positions may be closed. Breach data (rule, time, equity) is recorded and shown in your dashboard.

5. Funded accounts, rewards and scaling

  • After passing all phases of a Challenge, your account is granted "funded" status in the simulation.
  • You may request a reward equal to your profit split as stated on your plan (90% on evaluation plans, 70% on Instant Funding) of the simulated profits accrued on the funded account.
  • Reward requests require completed identity verification (KYC) and are reviewed by our team. We may refuse a reward where results were produced by prohibited conduct (Section 6).
  • Your first reward includes a refund of the Challenge fee for that account.
  • Funded accounts scale automatically: each time the account grows by +10%, its balance is increased by +25%.
