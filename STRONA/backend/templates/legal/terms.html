4. Challenge rules and how they are enforced

Each Challenge is governed by the objectives displayed on its plan card and on the Trading Objectives table at the time of purchase: profit targets, maximum daily loss, maximum overall loss (static drawdown on every plan we sell), minimum trading days and the cap on total open volume. Objectives of a Challenge you have already bought do not change.

  • The trading day runs on server time, which is {{ legal.server_utc }} all year round (it does not move with daylight-saving time). The daily loss limit resets at 00:00 server time.
  • The daily loss limit is measured on equity, which includes the floating result of open positions, against your equity at the start of the trading day.
  • The overall loss limit is measured on equity against the initial balance of the account.
  • A trading day is a server-time day on which the account had an open position.
  • A stop-out closes all positions when equity falls to 50% of used margin.

These rules are monitored and enforced automatically by our risk engine. If a rule is breached, the account is marked as failed and open positions are closed. The rule, the time and the equity value are recorded and shown in your dashboard. You can ask a member of our team to review a breach under the Complaints Procedure.

5. Funded accounts, rewards and scaling

  • By default you may submit a reward request every 14 calendar days (bi-weekly) on 2-Step and Instant Funding accounts. The optional On-demand Payout add-on ($299 on 2-Step, $399 on Instant Funding), when purchased with the account, removes that 14-day wait for that account.
  • Before the first reward on a funded account, the account must have the plan's minimum trading days on that funded stage: 5 on 2-Step and 30 on Instant Funding.
  • The minimum reward request is $500 or 2% of the account size, whichever is higher.
  • A reward request requires completed identity verification (KYC) and is reviewed by a member of our team before it is paid.
  • On 2-Step plans, your first reward on a funded account includes a credit of the fee you paid for that Challenge as additional vUSDT on that payout. It is a credit, not a refund to the original payment method. Instant Funding accounts carry no fee credit; an Instant Funding account purchased while the credit was offered on every plan keeps it.
  • Scaling is your choice, not an automatic upgrade. Each time a funded account grows by +15% you may either request the reward or move up to the next account size in our pricing. The two are mutually exclusive: if you move up, the accrued profit is applied to the larger account and no reward is paid for that period. Moving up replaces the account with a new simulated account of the larger size, funded from the start, subject to that plan's objectives and profit split. The minimum trading days before a reward start again on the new account. Scaling is available up to the largest size we offer.
