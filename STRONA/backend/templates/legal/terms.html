2. Simulated environment

All accounts provided under these Terms are demo accounts. All trading occurs in a simulated environment with fictitious funds. No real money is traded and your activity does not reach any real market. Amounts referred to as "capital", "balance", "profit" or "drawdown" describe values inside the simulation. Payments made to traders are discretionary performance-based rewards calculated from simulated results, not proceeds of real trading.

Prices shown in the Built-in Terminal and used to simulate fills are reference data from third-party venues and market-data providers, including Hyperliquid. We are not affiliated with any of them, no order is sent to them, and simulated fills may differ from what a real market would have given.

4. Challenges, rules and enforcement

Each Challenge is governed by the objectives displayed on its plan card and on the Trading Objectives table at the time of purchase: profit targets, maximum daily loss, maximum overall loss (static drawdown on every plan we sell), and minimum trading days. These rules are monitored and enforced automatically by our risk engine. If a rule is breached, the account is marked as failed and open positions may be closed. Breach data (rule, time, equity) is recorded and shown in your dashboard.

5. Funded accounts, rewards and scaling

  • After passing all phases of a Challenge, your account is granted "funded" status in the simulation.
  • You may request a reward equal to your profit split as stated on your plan (90% on evaluation plans, 70% on Instant Funding) of the simulated profits accrued on the funded account. Rewards are paid in vUSDT on the Polygon network.
  • vUSDT is {{ site_name }}'s native internal token on Polygon. It is not Tether USDT or any third-party stablecoin. Tokens are transferable on Polygon to a wallet you control. We do not redeem vUSDT for cash, card payouts or other fiat. vUSDT is a reward token only; its market value is not guaranteed and may vary.
  • By default you may submit a reward request every 14 calendar days (bi-weekly) on 2-Step and Instant Funding accounts. The optional On-demand Payout add-on ($299 on 2-Step, $399 on Instant Funding), when purchased with the account, removes that 14-day wait for that account.
  • Before the first reward on a funded account, the account must have the plan's minimum trading days on that funded stage: 5 on 2-Step and 30 on Instant Funding.
  • The minimum reward request is $500 or 2% of the account size, whichever is higher.
  • Reward requests require completed identity verification (KYC) and are reviewed by our team. We may refuse a reward where results were produced by prohibited conduct (Section 6).
  • On 2-Step plans, your first reward on a funded account includes a credit of that Challenge fee as additional vUSDT on that payout. It is a credit, not a refund to the original payment method. Instant Funding accounts carry no fee credit; an Instant Funding account purchased while the credit was offered on every plan keeps it.
  • Scaling is your choice, not an automatic upgrade. Each time a funded account grows by +15% you may either request the reward or move up to the next account size in our pricing. The two are mutually exclusive: if you move up, the accrued profit is applied to the larger account and no reward is paid for that period. Moving up replaces the account with a new simulated account of the larger size, funded from the start, subject to that plan's objectives and profit split. Scaling is available up to the largest size we offer.

  • copying trades between accounts, unless the account carries the Copytrading permission described below;
  • buying, selling or transferring accounts, or letting anyone else trade your account outside an approved management arrangement (Section 7);
  • fraud, chargeback abuse, identity misrepresentation or KYC falsification;

We may review an account before approving a reward, including its trading history and sign-in records. Where we find prohibited conduct, we may void the affected results and any reward based on them, and close the account.

The permission covers accounts registered to you. Copying to or from an account registered to someone else, and giving your credentials to another person, stay subject to this Section and to Section 7.
