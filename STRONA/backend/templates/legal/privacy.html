2. What we collect

  • Account data: e-mail, password (stored as a salted hash), full name, and, if you use "Continue with Google", your Google account identifier.
  • Country from your IP address: recorded when you register and when you log in.
  • Checkout data: first name, last name and phone number.
  • Proof of your declarations: which version of our documents you accepted, when, and from which IP address and browser, for registration and for every order.
  • KYC data: full name, country, date of birth, address, the type and number of your identity document, images of that document and a proof of residence, submitted by you before rewards are paid.
  • Reward wallet: the Polygon wallet address you save for rewards.
  • Trading data: your simulated orders and trades, equity and balance snapshots, rule-breach events and challenge progress.
  • Built-in Terminal sign-ins: IP address, approximate location derived from it (country, region, city), browser and device type, an identifier stored in your browser, and the time of each sign-in, including failed attempts.
  • Payment data: processed by our payment provider (Stripe); we store order metadata, never full card numbers.
  • Support chat and e-mail: the messages you send us, including those answered by the AI assistant.
  • Usage events: a record of actions in the portal (for example signing in, opening checkout, submitting verification).
  • Newsletter: your e-mail address, if you subscribe.
  • Contact forms on partner pages: if you leave your details in a form that refers to us, we receive your name, e-mail, phone number, messaging handle, country and your answers.
  • Push notifications: if you enable them, the subscription address your browser gives us.

3. What we use it for, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service (creating your account, running the simulation, monitoring objectives, paying rewards, answering support questions): performance of our contract with you.
  • Transactional e-mail and notifications (credentials, phase results, payout status): performance of the contract.
  • Identity verification before a reward: performance of the contract and our legitimate interest in paying rewards only to the real account holder; a legal obligation where anti-money-laundering or sanctions law applies to us.
  • Fraud prevention, account security and enforcing our Terms, including checking that an account is used by its registered owner: our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users.
  • Keeping proof of your declarations and records of orders and payouts: our legitimate interest in being able to establish and defend legal claims, and legal obligations (accounting and tax).
  • Marketing e-mail: your consent, given when you subscribe; you can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link.
  • Analytics and session replay (Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity): your consent, given in the cookie banner; you can withdraw it at any time in Cookie Preferences.
  • Remembering which affiliate referred you: your consent in Cookie Preferences.
  • Following up contact forms: steps taken at your request before a contract.

You do not have to give us your data, but without account, checkout and (before a reward) verification data we cannot provide the service or pay a reward.

We do not sell personal data and we do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

4. Automated decisions and risk checks

Whether a trading rule was breached is decided automatically by our risk engine from your simulated trading data; a breach fails the account. This is necessary to perform the contract, because the objectives are the service you bought. You can ask a member of our team to review any such decision, give your point of view and contest it, under the Complaints Procedure.

Before a reward is paid, our team reviews the request. To help that review we compute risk indicators from your data: whether the country of your sign-ins matches your registration and verification, how many devices and countries were used, whether an IP address is shared with another trader, and the region your account originates from (from IP address, phone prefix and verification country). These indicators are shown to the reviewer; the decision is made by a person, not by software alone.

5. Who receives your data

We share data only with providers that help us run the service, each for its own task:

  • Stripe (payments);
  • Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database hosting);
  • our e-mail delivery providers (currently Brevo and Resend);
  • Google, for "Continue with Google" sign-in, and, only if you accept Analytics, Google Analytics 4;
  • Microsoft (Clarity), only if you accept Analytics;
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile), to tell people from bots on some forms;
  • the AI model provider behind the support assistant (currently Anthropic), which receives the messages you type in the support chat;
  • Twilio (text messages), where you left a phone number in a contact form, and Telegram (internal notifications to our team about new contact requests and account events);
  • the provider of your browser's push service, if you enable push notifications;
  • where your account runs on a third-party trading platform, the provider of that platform (the account name on its demo server).

Market data in your browser. Charts and live prices in the Built-in Terminal are loaded by your browser directly from market-data providers (Hyperliquid, Binance and Bybit). They receive your IP address, as any website you connect to does; we do not send them your account details.

Public blockchain. A reward in vUSDT is a transfer on the Polygon network. The wallet address, the amount and the time are public and permanent, and we cannot delete them.

Certificates. A payout or pass certificate shows your name and can be opened by anyone who has its link. The public verification page shows the name in masked form.

We also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a court or an authority.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Several of these providers are based in the United States or process data there. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on a European Commission adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for providers certified under it) or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us which applies to a given provider.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: while your registration exists. When you delete your registration in the portal, your name, contact details, verification details and documents, reward wallet and Google link are erased or anonymised.
  • Identity documents: while your registration exists and you may request rewards; erased when you delete your registration, unless a law that applies to us requires us to keep them longer.
  • Orders, payouts and proof of your declarations: kept after the registration is deleted, for as long as accounting and tax law requires and legal claims can be brought.
  • Trading data of closed accounts: kept in a form no longer linked to your name, for record and certificate verification.
  • Built-in Terminal sign-in records: deleted after 12 months.
  • Support conversations: for as long as legal claims connected with them can be brought.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

9. Cookies and local storage

We set one cookie of our own: a session cookie (pf_session) when you log in, which is essential. Everything else the site keeps is in your browser's local or session storage:

  • Essential (needed for the site to work, no switch): your login token (pf_token), the sessions of accounts you sign in to in the Built-in Terminal and the device identifier recorded with those sign-ins (pf_term_logins, pf_term_acc, pf_term_dev), and your privacy choice (pf_consent).
  • Preferences (kept until you clear them): theme, terminal layout, drawings and watchlists (pf_theme2, pf_term_*), dismissed banners and prompts, a coupon you applied (pf_coupon_code, pf_promo_code), and which notifications and help messages you have already seen.
  • Referral attribution (optional): if you arrive through an affiliate link, the code is held for that browser tab only (pf_ref_pending). It is stored (pf_ref, for up to 90 days) only if you allow Referral attribution in Cookie Preferences, so that the partner can be credited.

Some pages load a script from a third party because the page cannot work without it: Google's sign-in button on the portal login page and Cloudflare Turnstile on forms it protects. These providers may set their own cookies needed for that function.

Analytics (optional — needs your consent)

If you click Accept all or enable Analytics under Cookie Preferences, we load:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — visit counts, page views and basic device / acquisition metrics. Google may set cookies and process technical identifiers under its own terms.
  • Microsoft Clarity — heatmaps and session replay (a recording of how you move, click and scroll on pages). This is more than a simple page counter. Clarity masks the contents of input fields, so passwords, personal details and payment data you type are not meant to be recorded. Microsoft may set cookies and process technical identifiers under its own terms.

Neither tool loads until you consent. With your consent they also run in the trader portal and the Built-in Terminal, not only on the public pages. You can change your mind anytime via Cookie Preferences. Declining or blocking them does not affect your account or the service. We do not sell this data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.
