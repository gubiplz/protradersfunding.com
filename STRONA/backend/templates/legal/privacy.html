Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account data: e-mail, password (stored as a salted hash), full name.
  • Checkout data: first name, last name, phone number, required to open your MT5 account with the platform provider.
  • KYC data: full name, country, date of birth, address, the type and number of your identity document, images of that document and a proof of residence, submitted by you before rewards are paid.
  • Reward wallet: the Polygon wallet address you save for rewards.
  • Trading telemetry: equity/balance snapshots, rule-breach events and challenge progress of your simulated accounts.
  • Built-in Terminal sign-ins: IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device type, an identifier stored in your browser, and the time of each sign-in, including failed attempts.
  • Payment data: processed by our payment provider (Stripe); we store order metadata, never full card numbers.
  • Support chat: the messages you send in the support chat, including those answered by the AI assistant.
  • Newsletter: your e-mail address, if you subscribe.

2. What we use it for

  • Providing the service: creating MT5 accounts, monitoring objectives, paying rewards.
  • Transactional e-mail (credentials, phase results, payout status).
  • Fraud prevention, keeping accounts secure and enforcing our Terms, including checking that an account is used by its registered owner.
  • Reviewing reward requests: the review uses your trading history, your verification and the Built-in Terminal sign-in records (IP address, approximate location, device). The decision is made by a member of our team, not by software alone.
  • Answering support questions, including through the AI assistant in the support chat.
  • Marketing e-mail, only if you subscribed; every message has an unsubscribe link.
  • Legal obligations (accounting, AML/KYC where applicable).

We do not sell personal data and we do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

3. Sharing

Data is shared only with processors necessary to run the service:

  • the MT5 infrastructure provider (account name on the demo server);
  • Stripe, our payment provider;
  • Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database hosting);
  • our e-mail delivery provider;
  • the AI model provider behind the support assistant, which receives the messages you type in the support chat;
  • only if you accept Analytics in the cookie banner: Google (Google Analytics 4) and Microsoft (Clarity).

Each processes data under its own terms / agreement with us.

4. Retention

Account and order data are kept for as long as your account exists and thereafter as required by law (typically accounting periods). Trading telemetry of closed accounts is retained for record and certificate verification purposes.

Built-in Terminal sign-in records are deleted after 12 months.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, rectify, export or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Write to {{ support_email }}. We respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

6. Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies of our own in your browser apart from the session cookie of the internal admin panel. The site keeps the following in your browser's local or session storage:

  • Essential (needed for the site to work, no switch): your login token (pf_token), the sessions of MT5 accounts you sign in to in the Built-in Terminal and the device identifier recorded with those sign-ins (pf_term_logins, pf_term_acc, pf_term_dev), and your privacy choice (pf_consent).
  • Preferences (kept until you clear them): theme, terminal layout, drawings and watchlists (pf_theme2, pf_term_*), dismissed banners and prompts, a coupon you applied (pf_coupon_code, pf_promo_code), and which notifications and help messages you have already seen.
  • Referral attribution (optional): if you arrive through an affiliate link, the code is held for that browser tab only (pf_ref_pending). It is stored (pf_ref, for up to 90 days) only if you allow Referral attribution in Cookie Preferences, so that the partner can be credited.

Analytics (optional — needs your consent)

If you click Accept all or enable Analytics under Cookie Preferences, we load:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — visit counts, page views and basic device / acquisition metrics. Google may set cookies and process technical identifiers under its own terms.
  • Microsoft Clarity — heatmaps and session replay (a recording of how you move, click and scroll on pages). This is more than a simple page counter. Clarity masks the contents of input fields, so passwords, personal details and payment data you type are not meant to be recorded. Microsoft may set cookies and process technical identifiers under its own terms.

Neither tool loads until you consent. With your consent they also run in the trader portal and the Built-in Terminal, not only on the public pages. You can change your mind anytime via Cookie Preferences. Declining or blocking them does not affect your account or the service. We do not sell this data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2), credentials are transmitted over TLS, and access to production data is restricted. No system is perfectly secure — report concerns to {{ support_email }}.

8. Contact

Data controller: {{ site_name }} (contact: {{ support_email }}).
