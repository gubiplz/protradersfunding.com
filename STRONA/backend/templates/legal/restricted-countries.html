1. Who can take part

  • You must be at least 18 years old, or older if the law where you live sets a higher age for entering into contracts.
  • You must not be resident in, or located in, a restricted country listed below.
  • You must not be subject to sanctions of the European Union, the United Nations or the United States (OFAC), and must not act for anyone who is.
  • You must be allowed to use a service like ours under the law of the place where you live. It is your responsibility to check this before you buy.

3. If a restriction applies to you

An account opened in breach of this page is closed. Where the only issue is that you bought from a restricted country, we refund the fee you paid for accounts on which no reward has been paid. No reward is paid to a person to whom a restriction applies.

Using a VPN, a proxy or false details to hide where you are is prohibited conduct under the Terms of Service.

4. Identity verification (KYC)

  • We verify your identity before any reward is paid. Verification opens when one of your accounts reaches the funded stage, or earlier if we ask for it.
  • You provide your full name, country, date of birth, address and identity document number, a photo of the document (front and back) and a proof of residence.
  • Verification is reviewed by a member of our team. We may ask you to correct or replace a document.
  • The name on the account, on the verification and on the reward wallet request must be yours. Rewards are paid only to the verified account holder.
  • If verification fails or is refused, no reward is paid. You can ask for the reason and complain under the Complaints Procedure.

How we store and protect these documents, and for how long, is described in the Privacy Policy.
