Refund Policy

Version {{ legal.version }} · Last updated: {{ legal.date }}

1. The fee, in short

A Challenge fee is a one-time payment for access to an evaluation program on a demo account. There are no recurring charges.

2. When you get the fee back

  • Before delivery: if you paid but your MT5 credentials have not yet been delivered, you may cancel for a full refund to the original payment method. See the Right of Withdrawal for how to do it and for the form.
  • Duplicate charges, and an account that we could not provision, are refunded in full to the method you paid with.
  • Service not as described: if the account you received does not work or is not what the plan card and these documents described, tell us under the Complaints Procedure. If we cannot put it right, the fee is refunded.
  • Restricted country: see Section 3 of the Restricted Countries page.

Fee credit on 2-Step plans (a credit, not a refund)

On 2-Step plans, when you pass the Challenge and receive your first reward on the funded account, the fee you paid for that account is credited as additional vUSDT on that payout. This is a performance-reward credit in vUSDT. It is not a refund to your card or other original payment method, and it depends on a first reward being approved. vUSDT is not money and may not be convertible into money (see the Risk Disclosure). Instant Funding accounts have no fee credit.

3. When the fee is not refundable

  • After your MT5 credentials have been delivered and trading is possible, the service is deemed performed in full. At checkout you ask us to start the service immediately and acknowledge that, where a right of withdrawal applies, you lose it once the service is fully performed.
  • Failing a Challenge (breaching a loss limit or otherwise not meeting the objectives) does not entitle you to a refund.
  • Accounts terminated for prohibited conduct under the Terms are not refunded.

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the mandatory consumer law of the country where you live.

4. Discounts and coupons

If a coupon was applied at checkout, any refund equals the amount actually paid, not the list price. Promotional discounts have no cash value on their own.

5. How refunds are processed

To request a refund, e-mail {{ support_email }} from your registered address with the order number. Approved amounts go back to the original payment method. A withdrawal under the Right of Withdrawal is refunded no later than 14 days from the day we receive it; how long the money then takes to appear depends on your bank or payment provider. The 2-Step Challenge fee credit on first reward is paid as additional vUSDT with that payout, not to your card.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a card dispute: a justified refund claim is honoured without one. While a payment for an account is disputed, we may suspend that account and hold any reward on it. If the dispute is decided in our favour, the account is restored. Disputing a payment for a Challenge that was delivered and used as described is treated as chargeback abuse under the Terms.
