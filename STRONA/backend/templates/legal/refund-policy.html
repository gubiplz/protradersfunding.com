Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. The fee, in short

A Challenge fee is a one-time payment for access to an evaluation program on a demo account. There are no recurring charges.

2. When you get the fee back

  • Before delivery: if you paid but your MT5 account has not yet been created, you may cancel for a full refund to the original payment method. Write to {{ support_email }}.
  • Money-back to the original payment method: duplicate charges, or an account that we could not provision, are refunded in full to the method you paid with.

Fee credit on 2-Step plans (a credit, not a refund)

On 2-Step plans, when you pass the Challenge and receive your first reward on the funded account, the full Challenge fee for that account is credited as additional vUSDT on that payout. This is a performance-reward credit in vUSDT. It is not a refund to your card or other original payment method, and it depends on a first reward being approved. Instant Funding accounts have no fee credit.

3. When the fee is not refundable

  • After your MT5 credentials have been delivered and trading is possible, the service is deemed performed in full. At checkout you ask us to start the service immediately and acknowledge that, where a right of withdrawal applies, you lose it once the service is fully performed.
  • Failing a Challenge (breaching a loss limit or otherwise not meeting the objectives) does not entitle you to a refund.
  • Accounts terminated for prohibited conduct under the Terms are not refunded.

4. Discounts and coupons

If a coupon was applied at checkout, any refund equals the amount actually paid, not the list price. Promotional discounts have no cash value on their own.

5. How refunds are processed

Where this policy provides a money-back refund (duplicates, provisioning failure, or cancellation before delivery), approved amounts go back to the original payment method; how long they take to appear depends on your bank or payment provider. The 2-Step Challenge fee credit on first reward is paid as additional vUSDT with that payout, not to your card. To request a money-back refund, e-mail {{ support_email }} from your registered address with the order number.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a card dispute. A chargeback is unwarranted when it disputes a Challenge that was delivered as described and was opened without contacting us first; unwarranted chargebacks lead to account termination under the Terms. Legitimate refund claims are honoured without a dispute.
