Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. The fee, in short

A Challenge fee is a one-time payment for access to an evaluation program on a demo account. There are no recurring charges.

2. When you get the fee back

  • Promo fee credit in vUSDT on first reward: when you pass the Challenge and receive your first reward on the funded account, the full Challenge fee for that account is credited as additional vUSDT on that payout. This is a performance-reward credit in vUSDT, not a refund to your card or other original payment method.
  • Before delivery: if you paid but your MT5 account has not yet been created, you may cancel for a full refund to the original payment method. Write to {{ support_email }}.
  • Money-back to the original payment method: duplicate charges, or an account that we could not provision, are refunded in full to the method you paid with.

3. When the fee is not refundable

  • After your MT5 credentials have been delivered and trading is possible, the service is deemed performed in full; starting the Challenge waives the standard withdrawal period to the extent permitted by law.
  • Failing a Challenge (breaching a loss limit or otherwise not meeting the objectives) does not entitle you to a refund.
  • Accounts terminated for prohibited conduct under the Terms are not refunded.

4. Discounts and coupons

If a coupon was applied at checkout, any refund equals the amount actually paid, not the list price. Promotional discounts have no cash value on their own.

5. How refunds are processed

Where this policy provides a money-back refund (duplicates, provisioning failure, or cancellation before delivery), approved amounts go back to the original payment method; how long they take to appear depends on your bank or payment provider. The Challenge fee credit on first reward is paid as additional vUSDT with that payout, not to your card. To request a money-back refund, e-mail {{ support_email }} from your registered address with the order number.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a card dispute — unwarranted chargebacks lead to account termination under the Terms. Legitimate refund claims are honoured without a dispute.
