Right of Withdrawal

Version {{ legal.version }} · Last updated: {{ legal.date }}

1. Your right

Where the law gives you, as a consumer, a right to withdraw from a purchase made online, you may withdraw within 14 days of the day you placed the order, without giving any reason, until the service has been fully performed.

2. When the right ends

At checkout you ask us to start the service immediately, so that your account is available as soon as the payment clears, and you acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once the service is fully performed. We treat the service as performed in full when your MT5 credentials have been delivered and trading is possible. Delivery is automatic right after payment, so in practice that window is very short. Until the credentials are delivered you can cancel for a full refund.

Where an order contains more than one account, this is assessed separately for each account.

3. How to withdraw

Send us a clear statement that you withdraw, while the right still applies, from the e-mail address registered on your account to {{ support_email }}. You may use the form below, but you do not have to. It is enough that you send the statement before the period ends.

4. What happens next

We confirm that we received your withdrawal. Where it is valid, we refund everything you paid for that account, using the same payment method you used, without undue delay and no later than 14 days from the day we received your statement. We do not charge any fee for the refund. The account is closed.

6. Other refunds

Refunds outside the right of withdrawal (duplicate charges, an account we could not deliver, a service that was not as described) are covered by the Refund Policy and the Complaints Procedure. Nothing on this page limits rights you have under the mandatory consumer law of the country where you live.
