FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these live trading accounts?
No. All accounts are demo accounts with virtual funds running in a simulated environment on real MetaTrader 5 servers. You are never trading real money — you earn performance-based rewards for passing objectives and trading profitably.
How fast do I get my account?
Account creation is fully automated. Your MT5 server, login and password usually appear in your dashboard (and inbox) within a minute of completing checkout.
How is the daily loss limit calculated?
The daily loss limit (5%) is measured against your equity at the start of the trading day (server time) and includes floating P&L. If your equity drops below the daily floor, the account fails automatically.
What is the difference between static and trailing drawdown?
Static drawdown is a fixed floor below your starting balance (e.g. 10% on 2-step plans). Trailing drawdown (1-step plans, 6%) follows your equity high-water mark up until it locks at breakeven. Each plan card states which type applies.
What happens if I break a rule?
The risk engine fails the account automatically, open positions are closed, and you'll see the exact breach reason in your dashboard. You can always start a new challenge.
Is there a limit on position size?
Yes — every account has a cap on the total volume of open positions (6 lots on a $100,000 account, scaled proportionally to account size). It exists so the challenge measures skill rather than one all-in trade. Exceeding it breaches the account, the same as a loss limit.
What is Instant Funding?
A funded account without an evaluation — you skip the phases and can request payouts from day one. In exchange the fee is higher, the risk limits are tighter (4% daily, 6% overall) and the profit split is 70% instead of 80–90%.
Is news trading allowed? Can I hold over the weekend?
Yes and yes. There are no news blackouts, no weekend-flat requirements and no banned strategies. The only rules are the loss limits and targets published on this page.
How do payouts work?
On a funded account you can request a payout of your share (80–90%) of the simulated profits at any time. After a one-time KYC verification, each request is reviewed by our team and paid out; your first payout also includes a full refund of the challenge fee.
Is the fee really one-time? Any hidden costs?
One-time, per challenge. No subscriptions, no data fees, no platform fees — and the fee is refunded in full with your first payout.
Which platform and instruments can I trade?
MetaTrader 5 — desktop, mobile and web terminal, with the same credentials. You can trade whatever the MT5 server offers: forex pairs, indices, gold and metals, and crypto CFDs.
Can I have multiple accounts?
Yes, you can run multiple challenges in parallel — each account is evaluated independently against its own objectives.
