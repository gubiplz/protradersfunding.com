FAQ

Common questions

What traders ask most before they start their first challenge.

Are these live trading accounts?
No. All accounts are demo accounts with virtual funds running in a simulated environment on real MetaTrader 5 servers. You are never trading real money. You earn performance-based rewards for passing objectives and trading profitably.
What are the challenge rules?
A profit target, a daily loss limit (5%) measured against your equity at the start of the trading day, a static overall drawdown (10% on 2-Step, 8% on Instant Funding) and a cap on the total open volume. No time limit, no news blackouts, no weekend-flat rule. Break a limit and the risk engine fails the account automatically.
What can I trade?
MetaTrader 5: desktop, mobile and web terminal, with the same credentials. You can trade whatever the MT5 server offers: forex pairs, indices, gold and metals, energies and crypto CFDs.
How do payouts work?
On a funded account you can request a payout of your share (90%, or 70% on Instant Funding) of the simulated profits at any time. After a one-time KYC verification, each request is reviewed by our team and paid out; your first payout also includes a full refund of the challenge fee.
How fast do I get my account?
Account creation is fully automated. Your MT5 server, login and password usually appear in your dashboard (and inbox) within a minute of completing checkout.
Is there a limit on how many challenges I can buy?
No. You can run multiple challenges in parallel, and each account is evaluated independently against its own objectives. The fee is one-time per challenge, with no subscriptions or hidden costs.
